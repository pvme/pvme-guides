## __Introduction & Disclaimer__
.tag:intro
This channel lists the recommended general melee purchase/unlock progression for players to follow.

**Last Revised: 23rd October 2026**
The order is **subject to change at any time** as prices change, updates occur, and knowledge expands

Please note the following assumptions:
⬥ The order assume a **starting point of T70 gear** <:dharokaxe:854475442182029382> <:bandoschest:643151021748518917>
⬥ **Priorities may be different** if you are doing niche content
⬥ "ROI" (meaning return-on-investment) typically means **quality-of-life upgrades or typical damage increase**
⬥ We do **not recommend selling an item to buy another**, unless it is a direct upgrade
⬥ **Unlisted items** are not worth buying

**Feel free to ask in <#656898197561802760> for a tailored list to the content that you do!**

.tag:necromancy
*For further information on each upgrade, check <#1025569571710718093>, <#553632787639435284> and <#689236234912137266>, <#1025569719056597042>, or <#1020550560551813131>*

### Kili's Tasks
Upon reaching level 70 you have the option to choose your path between power and tank armour. Completing either branch allows you to also create the same tier weaponry.

⬥ Death**dealer** <:dealerhood80:1142783479810117702> - experienced players
⬥ Death**warden** <:wardenhood80:1142782592878387321> - less experienced players
    • *Note: these tasks are generally easier, but slightly more time consuming*

.
### Stage 1: High ROI
.tag:stage1necro
⬥ Work towards everything in <#1242226798742208642>
⬥ Upgrade to T70 Weapons <:deathguard70:1138809262194294864> <:skulllantern70:1138809253759569970>
⬥ T70 Deathwarden (Tank Armour)
⬥ Craft the Deathwarden Nexus <:deathwardennexus:1179716442816532540>
⬥ Conjurer's Raising Amulet <:conjurersamulet:1254471154362744862> - <:coins:698816156961603654> $data_pvme:unlock_conjureraisingamulet$
⬥ Upgrade to T80 Weapons <:deathguard80:1138809249061929082> <:skulllantern80:1138809247308709988>
⬥ Scripture of Jas <:scriptureofjas:869284342839513108> - <:coins:698816156961603654> $data_pvme:unlock_jasbook$
⬥ Equilibrium 2 <:eq2:689502258424971564> - <:coins:698816156961603654> $data_pvme:perk_eq2$
⬥ Upgrade to T90 Weapons <:deathguard90:1138809243143766118> <:skulllantern90:1138809239255658587>
⬥ Pick one ring to rule them all (you don't need both):
    • Zorgoth's Soul Ring <:zorgothsring:1232124265826877574> - upgraded version of quest reward from [Requiem for a Dragon](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Requiem_for_a_Dragon/Quick_guide#Rewards>)
    • Occultist's Ring <:occultistring:1245442140318863490> - <:coins:698816156961603654> $data_pvme:unlock_occulistring$
