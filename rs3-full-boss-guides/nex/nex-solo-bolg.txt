## __Introduction__
.tag:intro
When Nex spawns for the first time in an instance she will be praying <:DeflectMage:544195487926845462> for the majority of the kill, making Ranged the meta for fast consistent kills. After every kill you will teleport out of the instance and re-enter in order to force Nex to always be praying <:DeflectMage:544195487926845462>.

*Note: It is strongly advised to complete the **Ritual of the Mahjarrat** quest in order to get access to the Stone of Jas <:jasboost:1006121672790773770> boost, which gives a +5% damage boost in GWD1 (including Nex). This can be done via Fairy ring code D.K.Q.*

.
