## __Rotations__
.tag:rotations
⬥ Abyssal Scourge <:abyssalscourge:947871842469834832> is **required** for this method to work

<:berserk:535532854004678657> → <:adrenrenewalflask:736298313980182541> or wait for 60% adren → <:overpowerigneous:959089455640215633> (giant mole will now dig twice, watch for her 2nd dig location)

<:surgemobile:867678153798975498> → <:divemobile:1049378665802235968> to where the giant mole is positioned

<:adaptivestrike:1478062906057949417> → <:chaosroar:1478062908767211640> mini moles inside tunnels on the way to giant mole

<:overpowerigneous:959089455640215633> → giant mole will now dig back to starting chamber

<:gbarge:535532879250456578> → <:assault:535532855191928842> → <:adaptivestrike:1478062906057949417> → <:punish:535532879439069184>

*Notes:*
⬥ *You may find that if the giant mole digs to the northwestern chamber first, she may not go back to the starting chamber after your <:overpowerigneous:959089455640215633>
    • This is due to the damage reduction mechanic.*
⬥ *If this happens you will need to follow her to a 2nd chamber and <:overpowerigneous:959089455640215633> which will guarantee her to dig to the starting chamber.*
