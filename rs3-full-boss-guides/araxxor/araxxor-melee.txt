## __Presets and Relics__
.tag:presets
⬥ [Melee preset](https://presets.pvme.io/?id=Me4GasJMYFjNy4aK0ScA) <:melee:1096130867279171706>

.
⬥ It is recommended to have the *[NopeNopeNope](<https://runescape.wiki/w/NopeNopeNope>)* perk from Player Owned Farms active
⬥ Upkeep the <:kalgscroll:841409588954923049> buff from your familiar
⬥ Araxxor takes a few seconds to clear vulns on phases 1–3, making vuln bombs <:vulnbomb:655341074235129858> usable on all phases

.tag:top
### Phase 1
⬥ Burn the web and use:

<:ezk:903244090953588787> <:spec:537340400273195028> → ~20s <:dba:603979368850653216> <:spec:537340400273195028> → ~35s <:dragonscimitar:1048592044731400252> <:spec:537340400273195028>

.
### Phase 2
⬥ At ~59 seconds, move directly through the path using:

<:powerburstofacceleration:756236265472524418> + <:dive:1049378668197195808> → <:surge:535533810004262912> → <:dive:1049378668197195808> → <:surge:535533810004262912> → <:surge:535533810004262912> → <:dive:1049378668197195808> → <:surge:535533810004262912> → <:surge:535533810004262912> → <:dive:1049378668197195808>

.
### Phase 3
<:chaosroar:994644356671737966> + <:vulnbomb:655341074235129858> → <:ezk:903244090953588787> <:spec:537340400273195028> (→ <:cane:535532878969438210> if not dead)

.
## __Middle Path__
.tag:mid
### Phase 1
⬥ Walk Araxxor against the web
⬥ At 99% web burn, use <:bd:535532854281764884> + <:surge:535533810004262912> into phase 2

.
### Phase 2
⬥ Run across the acid pool and stand 3 tiles up the ramp
⬥ Damage Araxxor until it has absorbed 53% acid
⬥ <:bd:535532854281764884> + <:surge:535533810004262912> up the platform and walk 1 tile west
⬥ Use <:nat:535541258131865633> when the acid bar reaches approximately 24–19%
⬥ Build to 100% adrenaline for phase 3

.
### Phase 3
<:zerk:535532854004678657> + <:adrenrenewal:736298121704767538> as you jump down the ramp → <:gbarge:535532879250456578> + <:vulnbomb:655341074235129858> → <:adaptivestrike:1478062906057949417> → <:assault:535532855191928842> → <:cane:535532878969438210> → <:punish:535532879439069184>

.
## __Bottom Path__
.tag:bot
### Phase 1
⬥ Damage Araxxor to below 5,000 LP

.
### Phase 2
⬥ Follow the light and remain at 100% adrenaline
⬥ During the charge, use a defensive ability to remain in combat
⬥ Use <:zerk:535532854004678657> + <:adrenrenewal:736298121704767538> while moving towards phase 3

.
### Phase 3
<:gbarge:535532879250456578> + <:vulnbomb:655341074235129858> → <:adaptivestrike:1478062906057949417> → <:assault:535532855191928842> → <:cane:535532878969438210> → <:punish:535532879439069184>

.
