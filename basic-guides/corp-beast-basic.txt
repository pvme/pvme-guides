## __Fight Mechanics__
.tag:fightMechanics
⬥ Any weapon type but spears will deal half damage, so use of a spear is highly recommended for learning - [click here](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Spears#Types_of_spears>) for a list.
⬥ Familiars will be killed (eaten, actually!), but legendary pets *are* allowed.
⬥ Protection prayers work, but have reduced effect.

.
### __Auto Attacks__
**Mechanic:**
Uses magic at range, and both magic and melee in melee distance.
Magic attacks hit in a 3x3 area of effect.

**Solution:**
⬥ Tank the damage and pray <:deflectmage:544195487926845462> / <:protectfrommagic:1052551456835768351> or
⬥ Use defensives <:debil:535541278264393729> <:reflect:535541258786177064> to reduce damage taken

.
### __Dark Core__
**Mechanic:**
A dark core spawns and follows you, dealing constant typeless damage and healing the boss.

**Solution:**
⬥ Keep moving while it's active
⬥ Kill the core when convenient to prevent the boss healing

.
