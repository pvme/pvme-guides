## __Introduction__
The aim of this guide is to provide a basic overview of how to get consistent kills at the Ghrazi Blood Knights. Due to demanding damage checks and mechanical complexity of this fight, contents of this guide assume you have at least made it to the end of Stage 1 of <#1020045708746833930> for your chosen style. As Ghrazi Blood Knights are enrage-based with fixed enrages of 100, 500, 750, 1000, 2000 and 4000%, mechanics and strategies may differ based on enrage. For the purposes of this guide, 100% enrage on **duo** mode will be utilized.

.
## __General Information__
.tag:generalInfo
⬥ Ghrazi Blood Knights are located in Havenhythe. Fighting them requires the completion of the [Heralds of Crimson](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Heralds_of_Crimson>) quest.
⬥ Both bosses share a health bar, with health based on chosen mode and enrage:
    • **Normal Mode**: 1.5M + 300k
    • **100%**: 2M + 600k
    • **500%**: 3M + 900k
    • **750%**: 3.5M + 1.05M
    • **1000%**: 4M + 1.2M
    • **2000%**: 5M + 1.5M
⬥ As of November 9th, Ghrazi Blood Knights can be fought in **solo** and **duo**.

.
## __Gear, Relics, Familiars__

The following presets are based on Stage 1 of <#1020045708746833930> for your chosen style. Antisanguine is strongly recommended, as it will apply a 40% damage reduction against all Sanguine damage dealt in this encounter, including Lord Vasily Ghrazi's mechanics.

## __Fight Mechanics (General)__
.tag:mechanics
As you are fighting two bosses at the same time, each player will have to handle the mechanics of their respective boss, barring shared mechanics.

.
### __Attack Rotation__
⬥ Karmina attacks with <:melee:1096130867279171706>, Vasily attacks with <:magic:689504724159823906>, with a mechanic every 4 auto-attacks for both

.
### __Close Formation__
⬥ If Karmina and Vasily are standing next to each other, they gain a stacking <:defence:689509250979987525> buff that reduces hit chance

.
### __Crimson Lament__ (Vasily) / __Blood Challenge__ (Karmina)
⬥ Both Karmina and Vasily gain a buff every time they hit their target, increasing all incoming damage from them by 4% per stack
    • Capped at 24 stacks, for a total of 96% increased damage
⬥ Stacks fall off after 30 seconds of not being attacked by the Blood Knight that inflicted the debuff (e.g. not being attacked by Vasily while having stacks of Crimson Lament)

.
### __Sanguine Influence__
⬥ Persistent debuff that increases all incoming damage from both bosses
⬥ Stacks are gained at the start of Phase 2 and throughout Phase 3

*Note: The current damage boost% per stack is unknown*

.
### __Isolated__
⬥ Persistent debuff that prevents players from directly benefitting from Heal Other, Heal Group, or Intercept
⬥ Only appears in 1000% Enrage or higher

.
### __Mechanics Rotation__
⬥ Both Karmina and Vasily perform 4 auto attacks between each mechanic:
    • Cross Cut -> Blood Waves & Sanguine Surge/Lunge -> Blood Bomb & Sanguine Strike/Blood Tribute -> repeat (Phase 1)

.
### __Cross Cut__ (a.k.a. Prayer Flurry)
⬥ Both Karmina and Vasily perform a flurry of attacks with all 3 styles, order of incoming attacks are random and the length is based on current phase
    • Performs 3 attacks in Phases 1-3
        ⬩ This is increased to 5 attacks in Phase 4 onwards
⬥ Each hit deals moderate to high damage if not prayed against, and only 1 damage if prayed against
⬥ All hits are within a tick of each other
    • The flurry arrives sooner in 500% Enrage and above

**Solution:**
⬥ Pray against the attacks accordingly:
    • __Red projectile:__ Pray <:melee:1096130867279171706>
    • __Green projectile:__ Pray <:ranged:580168050121113623>
    • __Blue projectile:__ Pray <:magic:689504724159823906>

*Note: The order of Cross Cut hits from Karmina and Vasily are the same*

.
### __Sanguine Surge__ (a.k.a. Lunge) (Karmina)
⬥ Karmina performs a hard hitting lunge that deals typeless damage and stuns <:stunicon:841419289428492369> the player

**Solution:**
⬥ <:surge:535533810004262912> / <:dive:1049378668197195808> / <:escape:535541258832052231> out of the way to fully negate the mechanic, timing is roughly 2 ticks after Karmina says `Nowhere is beyond Our reach!`
    • <:anti:535541306475151390> to prevent being stunned if caught off guard

*Note: Sanguine Surge does NOT reset movement abilities on a successful dodge*

.
### __Blood Waves__ (Vasily)
⬥ Vasily unleashes waves of blood that sweep parts of the arena, dealing <:magic:689504724159823906> damage
    • Number of blood waves depend on enrage, but all are 6 tiles wide and on Vasily's target

**Solution:**
⬥ Dodge the waves with <:surge:535533810004262912> / <:dive:1049378668197195808>, taking care not to accidentally hit the Karmina player
⬥ <:magic:689504724159823906> damage can be prayed against, and fully negated with <:devo:513190158728953857>

.
### __Sanguine Strike__ (a.k.a. Blood Tribute) (Karmina)
⬥ Karmina inflicts a hard hitting bleed that deals <:magic:689504724159823906> damage for 10.2s
    • <:freedom:535541258240786434> will **not** clear the bleed

**Solution:**
⬥ <:debil:535541278264393729> Karmina or <:reflect:535541258786177064> to mitigate incoming damage
    • Alternatively, both players switch targets and <:voke:535541259465392143> their respective new Blood Knights (e.g. Karmina player switches to Vasily, and vice versa)
        ⬩ This allows <:devo:513190158728953857> to fully negate the bleed as well as Vasily's attacks, removing KO potential from getting off-styled

*Note: It's possible to fully pray against all of the Sanguine Strike hits, but it's usually safer to burn a threshold defensive than to flick prayers*

.
### __Blood Bomb__ (a.k.a. Meatball) (Vasily)
⬥ Vasily unleashes a blob of Sanguine that deals typeless damage as it bounces six times between both players, starting with the player who has Vasily' aggression
⬥ Damage dealt by the blob increases based on distance

**Solution:**
⬥ Stick together and <:reflect:535541258786177064> + <:shielddome:537341954392850442> **or** <:cade:535541306353778689> to mitigate incoming damage
    • In 750% Enrage or less, <:cept:543478434509357098> <:cade:535541306353778689> will drastically limit incoming damage

.
## __Fight Mechanics (Phase 2)__
⬥ The Knights gain 200 Sanguine Influence stacks, increasing their damage dealt
⬥ Vasily gains a new mechanic exclusive to this phase, Tumour Summon

.
### __Mind Flail__ (a.k.a. Tumour Summon) (Vasily)
⬥ Vasily summons tumours visible only to the player who has his aggression
⬥ The tumours deal additional damage if allowed to fully channel

**Solution:**
⬥ Clicking on a tumour will __expunge__ it, negating the mechanic if all tumours are expunged

.
## __Fight Mechanics (Phase 3)__
⬥ Both Karmina and Vasily go Immune and six __Sanguine tumours__ spawn across the arena, and a 20 second timer begins as soon as they spawn
⬥ To progress to the next phase, the players must destroy as many __Sanguine tumours__ in the main arena as they can, then destroy the __Sanguine heart__

.
### __Sanguine Heart & Tumours__
⬥ Six __Sanguine Tumours__ litter the arena, each dealing typeless damage every 6s while the player is on the ground
⬥ After 20 seconds, the players are sent upstairs to the __Sanguine heart__, the duration they're staying based on how many tumours they have killed beforehand
    • Blood Knights also start channeling the heart once the players are sent up, adding __Sanguine Influence__ stacks every tick
⬥ The heart inflicts stacks of __Sanguine Haemmorhage__ every 12s, dealing damage over time

***Solution:***
⬥ Kill as many tumours as you can with strong AoE abilities, killing all 6 will send you up straight away
⬥ Destroy the heart as quickly as possible to limit the number of Sanguine Influence stacks the Blood Knights gain

.
### __Blood Diamond__ (Vasily)
⬥ Vasily summons 2 waves of hard hitting blasts that obliterate a large, diamond shaped area with typeless damage, first blast is smaller than the second

**Solution:**
⬥ The blasts are telegraphed, move out of the way, use <:dive:1049378668197195808> if necessary
    • Can run 2 tiles diagonally from starting position (and back) to avoid the blasts, preserving <:dive:1049378668197195808>

.
## __Fight Mechanics (Phase 4)__
⬥ After destroying the __Sanguine Heart__, players continue fighting the Blood Knights until their health fall to 1
⬥ Vasily gains a new mechanic during Phase 4

.
### __Sanguine Spores & Arena Destruction__ (Vasily)
⬥ Vasily starts charging and infests players with spores that, once on the ground, inflicts persistent <:magic:689504724159823906> damage over time
    • A secondary bar will charge above the players' heads indicating when the spores will be placed down on the arena
⬥ Once Vasily finishes charging, some parts of the arena starts glowing red, indicating that they will be ripped away

**Solution:**
⬥ Stand on the glowing tiles that are about to be ripped out and drop the spores there, then **move out of the way** to avoid a hard instakill <:instakill:1104794644497309809>

.
## __Fight Mechanics (Phase 5)__
⬥ Instead of dying, the Blood Knights become enraged, regaining some of their health and starting to inflict the __Exsanguinated__ debuff on the players
⬥ They will also gain a persistent vampyric lifesteal effect during this phase
⬥ To win the fight, the players must lower the health of the Blood Knights to 0

.
### __Exsanguinated__
⬥ Persistent, stacking debuff that builds over time by Karmina and Vasily's attacks and mechanics, increasing all incoming and outgoing damage
    • More stacks are inflicted if a player mis-handles a mechanic(?)
⬥ At 100 stacks, the team __**WIPES**__ <:instakill:1104794644497309809>

**Solution:**
⬥ Use Extra Action button to spawn a cleansing dome that removes Exsanguinated stacks

.
