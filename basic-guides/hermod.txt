## __General Information__
.tag:generalInfo
⬥ Hermod is a lieutenant of Rasial, the First Necromancer. He is a phantom born out of the unending conflicts of the God Wars in the Third Age, and continuously reforms himself when destroyed as long as wars are waged on Gielinor.
⬥ You must complete the [Spirit of War](<https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Spirit_of_War>) quest to fight Hermod.
⬥ Hermod has 20,000 LP for each player present in the instance, up to a maximum of 1,000,000 (with 5 players). Each player will get their own drop.
⬥ Hermod is immune to all combat styles except Necromancy.

## __Fight Mechanics__
.tag:fightMechanics
⬥ Hermod only has two mechanics, other than basic Necromancy attacks.

.
### __Smash__ [(Example)](<https://img.pvme.io/images/5T24rmqFUK.gif>)
**Mechanic:**
Ths boss will raise his sword over his head and smash it into your current location.

**Solution:**
⬥ Move at least two tiles from where you were standing **or**
⬥ Tank the attack

.
### __Conjure__ [(Example)](<https://img.pvme.io/images/nnVYF8mogS.gif>)
**Mechanic:**
The boss will raise his left hand and clench his fist. This will summon two armored phantoms with low health. Until these armored phantoms are dead Hermod is become immune to damage.

**Solution:**
⬥ Utilize AoE abilities such as <:bloodsiphon:1159434279311380532> or <:threadsoffate:1137809172335951933> to damage both minions at once.
