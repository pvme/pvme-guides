## __General Information__
.tag:generalInfo
⬥ Chaos Elemental is a very simple, beginner-friendly boss located west of Rogues' Castle in the far north of the Wilderness.
⬥ The boss is designed more to disrupt the player than deal high damage, mainly through forced movement and gear disruption.
⬥ Be safe - **toggle PvP off** at [Vala](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Vala>) in Edgeville before attempting this boss.
⬥ Keeping your inventory full prevents one of its main specials, making the fight even simpler.

## __Fight Mechanics__
.tag:fightMechanics
### __Discord__ [(Example)](<https://img.pvme.io/images/8OQ7OtVZhR.mp4>)
**Mechanic:**
Chaos Elemental's basic attack, made up of green particles, deals either <:magic:689504724159823906>, <:range:580168050121113623> or <:melee:1096130867279171706> damage. Its other attacks are variations of this move with different effects. Its Magic attack is the most accurate.

**Solution:**
⬥ Pray against Magic attacks <:protectfrommagic:1052551456835768351> <:deflectmage:544195487926845462> or use Soul Split <:soulsplit:615613924506599497>

.
### __Madness__ [(Example)](<https://img.pvme.io/images/gNKxb57aQz.mp4>)
**Mechanic:**
Chaos Elemental launches a multicoloured attack that unequips your main-hand weapon and up to 3 other equipped items, placing them into your inventory.

**Solution:**
⬥ Keep your inventory full to block this attack completely.
⬥ Pies are especially useful if you need food, as they leave a Pie Dish behind.
⬥ Picked-up drops can also help fill empty spaces.

.
### __Confusion__ [(Example)](<https://img.pvme.io/images/8OQ7OtVZhR.mp4>)
**Mechanic:**
Chaos Elemental launches a fire-based attack that teleports you up to around 20 tiles away in a random direction.

**Solution:**
⬥ After being teleported, click the boss again to resume attacking.
