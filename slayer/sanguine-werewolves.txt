## __Introduction__
.tag:intro
[Sanguine werewolves](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sanguine_werewolf>) are a low level Slayer <:slayer:797896049548066857> mob found in [Berylbrook](https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>), located in Havenhythe. They are mainly killed for the [Havenhythe collection log](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Havenhythe>).

## __Location and How to Get there__
.tag:location
Sanguine werewolves can be fought in [Berylbrook](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>) or the [Sunken Pyramid](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid>).

**Berylbrook**
.img:https://img.pvme.io/images/mx0A7JlRmi.png
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in [Berylbrook](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>), use a <:cannon:869283142350041098> as shown above.
    • While standing on the path, line yourself up with the blue fish on the clothesline then place the cannon 2 tiles north and then move back.
    • You may have to get them unstuck from the clothesline for the first kill, thus allowing them to get pulled from the side by the cannon.


.
**Sunken Pyramid**
.img:https://img.pvme.io/images/mGGioEKQkA.png
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in the [Sunken Pyramid](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid>), use a <:slayercodex:1097517249499254875>
    • May instead use <:houseteleportfree:1534426786048249907> and select Menaphos as your exit destination.
    • Place 5 Sanguine Werewolves in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.

.
