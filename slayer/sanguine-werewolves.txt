*Note: a **Table of Contents** can be found in the pins.*

## __Introduction__
.tag:intro
[Sanguine werewolves](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sanguine_werewolf>) are a low level Slayer <:slayer:797896049548066857> mob found in [Berylbrook](https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>), located in Havenhythe. They are mainly killed for the [Havenhythe collection log](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_collection_log#Havenhythe>).

.
### Stats
⬥ <:slayer:797896049548066857> level: 1
    • Level 99 Slayer <:slayer:797896049548066857> is required to access the Sunken Pyramid
⬥ <:slayer:797896049548066857> XP per kill: 1,877.5
⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~650 <:melee:1096130867279171706> (Sunken Pyramid)
⬥ Optimal <:slayer:797896049548066857> XP per hour: ~1.22m <:melee:1096130867279171706> (Sunken Pyramid)

## __Location and How to Get there__
.tag:location
Sanguine werewolves can be fought in [Berylbrook](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>) or the [Sunken Pyramid](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid>).

**Berylbrook**
.img:https://img.pvme.io/images/mx0A7JlRmi.png
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in [Berylbrook](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Berylbrook>), use a <:cannon:869283142350041098> as shown above.
    • While standing on the path, line yourself up with the blue fish on the clothesline then place the cannon 2 tiles north and then move back.
    • You may have to get them unstuck from the clothesline for the first kill, thus allowing them to get pulled from the side by the cannon.

.
**Sunken Pyramid**
.img:https://img.pvme.io/images/mGGioEKQkA.png
⬥ If killing sanguine werewolves in the [Sunken Pyramid](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Sunken_Pyramid>), use a <:slayercodex:1097517249499254875>
    • May instead use <:houseteleportfree:1534426786048249907> and select Menaphos as your exit destination.
    • Place 5 Sanguine Werewolves in the small room, and position yourself 7 tiles south of the northern statue.

.
## __Method: Berylbrook AFK__
.tag:berylbrook
Before loading your <:cannon:869283142350041098> and starting the session, check the following:

⬥ Pray <:deflectmelee:544195488447201300> and <:turmoil:583429936606347280> or <:malevolence:513190159416557573>
    • Sustain <:prayer:1005450651347730533> with <:powderofpenance:928221126360969226> and use <:powderofprotection:933079947419983912>
    • Using <:malevolence:513190159416557573> will require extra sustain.
⬥ Ensure <:elderovlsalve:648976643687317532> and <:weppoison:689525476158472288> are active at all times.
    • Upkeeping either <:superantisanguine:1555704881321353257> or <:extremeantisanguine:1555705025194623026> is required if using <:havensilverhalberd2:1555692252213547139>
⬥ Upkeep <:vampyrismaspect:1494069423047442573>
⬥ Activate <:vampscrim:513201294262009874>
⬥ Use armour perked with <:dev4:712073087713280033> or <:enhdev4:712073087507628035>
⬥ Set [Scavenge](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Scavenge>) (legendary pet) to "miscellaneous" only.

## __Method: Sunken Pyramid AFK__
.tag:pod
Before starting the session, check the following:

**Magic**
⬥ Pray <:soulsplit:615613924506599497> and <:torment:583429936958930964> or <:affliction:513190158468907008>
    • Sustain <:prayer:1005450651347730533> with <:powderofpenance:928221126360969226>
⬥ Ensure <:elderovlsalve:648976643687317532>, <:aggressionpotion:925794592199147581> and <:weppoison:689525476158472288> are active at all times.
⬥ Upkeep <:animatedead:856635090453135382> and autocast <:exsanguinate:856635090745950258>
⬥ Activate <:scriptureofwen:883134307902816297>
⬥ Set [Scavenge](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Scavenge>) (legendary pet) to "miscellaneous" only.

**Melee**
⬥ Pray <:deflectmelee:544195488447201300> and <:turmoil:583429936606347280> or <:malevolence:513190159416557573>
    • Sustain <:prayer:1005450651347730533> with <:penanceaspect:1494069332584693810> and use <:powderofprotection:933079947419983912>
⬥ Ensure <:elderovlsalve:648976643687317532>, <:aggressionpotion:925794592199147581> and <:weppoison:689525476158472288> are active at all times.
    • <:vampyrismaspect:1494069423047442573> is required if using <:havensilverhalberd2:1555692252213547139>
        ⬩ Also requires a different form of prayer restoration <:powderofpenance:928221126360969226>
⬥ Activate <:vampscrim:513201294262009874>
⬥ Use armour perked with <:enhdev4:712073087507628035>
⬥ Set [Scavenge](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Scavenge>) (legendary pet) to "miscellaneous" only.
