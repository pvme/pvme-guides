*Note: a **Table of Contents** can be found in the pins.*

## __Introduction__
.tag:intro
Abyssal savages serve as a good pre-105 slayer alternative, offering high combat experience and <:abyssalflesh:947871842520170576> drop rates.

.
### Stats
⬥ <:slayer:797896049548066857> level: 95
⬥ <:slayer:797896049548066857> XP per kill: 423.2
⬥ Optimal kills per hour: ~2668 <:melee:1096130867279171706> (Wilderness) / ~? <:magic:689504724159823906> / ~? <:necromancy:1148995625896120460>
⬥ Optimal <:slayer:797896049548066857> XP per hour: ~1.13M <:melee:1096130867279171706> (Wilderness) / ~? <:magic:689504724159823906> / ~? <:necromancy:1148995625896120460>

.
## __Desirable drops__
.tag:drops
⬥ Abyssal Flesh <:abyssalflesh:947871842520170576>, which is required to craft Abyssal armour spikes <:abyssalarmourspike:1004107347259555981>.
⬥ If killed in the Wilderness, they can also drop <:revenantdropenhancer:1006481325076787240> which can be helpful for Ultimate Slayer hunters.

## __Method - AFK (<:melee:1096130867279171706> / <:magic:689504724159823906> / <:necromancy:1148995625896120460>)__
.tag:method1
Before loading your cannon and starting the session, check that:
⬥ <:elderovl:841419289831800882>, <:aggressionpotion:925794592199147581>, <:weppoison:689525476158472288>, and <:vampscrim:513201294262009874> / <:scriptureofful:902209626412560395> / <:scriptureofwen:883134307902816297> are active.
⬥ Sustain <:prayer:1005450651347730533> with <:demonhornnecklace:975765831248130079> <:ectoplasmator:1023152065431744542> or <:powderofpenance:928221126360969226> / <:penanceaspect:1494069332584693810>
    • Prefer <:animatedead:856635090453135382> / <:darkness:1137809209782698024> over <:penanceaspect:1494069332584693810> when instructed or if extra survivability is needed.

.
### Prayers <:prayer:1005450651347730533>

⬥ <:melee:1096130867279171706> Pray <:eclipsedsoul:1285372688457797672>, <:divinerage:1285372247665803264>, <:ultimatestrength:1344313898068738151>, and <:steelskin:1365820216545902642> with <:zealots:926021352626982942>
    • Add Incredible Reflexes if accuracy is below 100%.
    • Sustain <:prayer:1005450651347730533> with <:penanceaspect:1494069332584693810>

⬥ <:magic:689504724159823906> / <:necromancy:1148995625896120460> Pray <:soulsplit:615613924506599497> and t95/t99 curse for the style you are using.

Magic <:magic:689504724159823906>
⬥ Autocast <:incitefear:856635090783567902>
⬥ Ensure that <:animatedead:856635090453135382> is active
    • <:graspingpouchblack:1390439136875253810> - <:bloodrune:536252658970001409> <:astralrune:536252658961481769> <:bodyrune:536252659301089280> <:soulrune:536252660333019136>
