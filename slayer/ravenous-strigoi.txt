## __Introduction__
.tag:intro
[Ravenous Strigoi](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Ravenous_strigoi>) are large beasts located in [Shuruk-Ba](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Shuruk-Ba>). They are slayer monsters that require the completetion of [Heralds of Crimson](<https://runescape.wiki/w/Heralds_of_Crimson>) to kill.

.

### __Rotation__
Once the cannon is placed, stay near the large wall and use it to avoid attacks from the west Strigois while simultaneously walking them into AoE range of each other. The cannon should be able to tag all 4 Strigois in the area if placed correctly. From there, alternate <:gdeathsswift:994644354536837121> + <:gsunshine:994644352871714836> ultimates while keeping an eye on your adrenaline buff <:critbuff:1279516151423963166> duration to end on full adrenaline for the next style. Use <:decimation:643848618477879328> <:eofspec:1257438999794946099> + <:gchain:1478063106914779166> to capitalise on any Strigoi you manage to lure close to each other.
